Logo plate

The only light surface in the system. It exists so company logos sit on the white they were drawn for, and for no other reason.

  • Logos are never altered. No recolouring, no monochrome treatment, no mask.
  • Seated into its card with an inset shadow and a hairline ring, both mixes of --plate-ink, so the plate reads as set into the surface rather than laid on it.
  • Inside a card the plate takes an exact height, not a minimum. Some logos have no intrinsic aspect ratio and resolve a fraction taller, which is enough to put a card out of step with its neighbours.
  • --plate-ink is the only ink token for a light surface, and the contrast checker asserts that the dark inks fail on it.
